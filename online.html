Study between contracts, or on board.

Three ways to learn online

The right mode depends on the course: some are fully online, others pair online theory with practical sessions in Cebu or Manila.

  • Learning platform
  • Course partner, Australian Maritime College
  • Enrollment and verification

Computer-based training

Self-paced modules you can complete on a laptop at home or on board, with assessments built in.

Blended learning

Theory online first, then a shorter classroom or simulator session at an Oceanwide branch to complete the practical part.

Distance learning

Instructor-led programs delivered remotely for companies that need to train crews spread across a fleet.

Oceanwide Online, on Seaversity

Oceanwide's learning management system runs on Seaversity. Enrolled trainees log in to take their modules, track progress and sit online assessments.

Trainees receive access when they enroll. The training office can help if you cannot log in.

Built with the Australian Maritime College

In June 2020 Oceanwide signed a memorandum of understanding with AMC Search, the training and consultancy division of the Australian Maritime College. By September the two had agreed to produce online courses together for Filipino seafarers.

The partnership brings AMC Search's interactive, scenario-based online training to the Philippine market, delivered and supported by Oceanwide.
