Your maritime solutions partner.

Founded in Cebu in 2012, Oceanwide surveys, audits and certifies ships on behalf of the Philippine Administration and trains the seafarers and port personnel who keep them running.

One partner for compliance, from the survey to the certificate.

Oceanwide is authorized by the Philippine agencies that implement the IMO conventions and domestic maritime law to act on their behalf: conducting statutory surveys and audits, and issuing the certificates that follow. We are also a MARINA-accredited STCW training institution.

Our team of naval architects, marine engineers, master mariners, instructors, assessors and technical staff works for ship owners and other stakeholders across the Philippines and the Asia Pacific, with tailor-made services and outcomes-based training.

We work alongside our sister companies in the Oceanwide Group: Oceanwide Surveys Inc. for survey, vetting and investigation, and Oceanskipper for compass adjusting and safety equipment servicing.

What the Administration authorizes us to do

As a Recognized Organization, Oceanwide carries out these statutory services on the Administration's behalf.

  • Philippine Coast GuardMARPOL survey for the issue and endorsement of the Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate and the Sewage Pollution Prevention Certificate.
  • Office for Transportation SecurityMARSEC audit for the National Ship Security Certificate and the Statement of Compliance of a Port Facility.
  • Maritime Industry AuthorityUnderwater survey of Philippine-registered ships to issue a Certificate of Inspection or extend its validity.
  • Maritime Industry AuthorityAccredited maritime training institution for STCW and domestic (MDS) courses.
  • Bureau VeritasISO 9001 certified quality management system.
  • National Privacy CommissionRegistered data protection officer and data processing systems.
