We inspect the part of the ship nobody sees.

As a MARINA-accredited underwater surveyor, our divers examine plating, propeller and rudder in the water, so a Philippine-registered ship can be issued or extend its Certificate of Inspection without going into dry dock.

It is one of the statutory jobs the Philippine Administration trusts us to do on its behalf, carried out by naval architects, marine engineers and master mariners.

How each survey and audit works

Training that meets the convention, and the ship

[ 01/05 ]

  1. STCW deck and engine

    Ratings forming part of a navigational watch and of a watch in a manned engine room, trained on full mission bridge and engine room simulators. Medical first aid included.

  2. Passenger ship safety

    Crowd management, crisis management and human behaviour, and safety training for crew who serve passengers directly.

  3. Maritime security

    Ship Security Officer, security awareness and designated security duties at sea, plus OTS-accredited PFSO, CSO and port facility courses in Cebu and Manila.

  4. Domestic trade (MDS)

    Deck and engine watchkeeping, safe navigation and collision regulations, Ship Handling and Safe Navigation 1 to 3, and Engine Operation and Maintenance 1 to 3.

  5. Online and blended

    Self-paced courses on Seaversity, programs built with AMC Search of the Australian Maritime College, and blended learning through Upright Systems.
