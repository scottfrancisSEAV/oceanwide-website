How to enroll

Enrollment runs through Oceanwide's online portal. The training office confirms each batch.

  1. Choose a course

    Find it above, or ask us to build an in-house course for your crew.

  2. Enroll online

    Search the course in the enrollment portal and submit your details.

  3. Confirm your batch

    The training office confirms the schedule, fees and requirements by email or phone.

  4. Attend and get certified

    Complete the course in Cebu or Manila. Your certificate is verifiable online.
