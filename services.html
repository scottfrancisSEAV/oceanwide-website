MARPOL survey and certification

Marpol Survey Organization of the Philippine Coast Guard

As a Marpol Survey Organization of the Philippine Administration for the MARPOL Convention, we survey ships for the issue and endorsement of:

  • Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate (OPPC)
  • Sewage Pollution Prevention Certificate (SPPC)
Request a MARPOL survey

MARSEC audit and certification

Recognized Security Organization of the Office for Transportation Security

As a Recognized Security Organization of the Philippine Administration for the ISPS Code, we audit ships and ports for:

  • National Ship Security Certificate (NSSC) for Philippine-registered ships
  • Statement of Compliance of a Port Facility (SoCPF)

We are also an OTS-accredited MARSEC training school for seafarers and port facility personnel.

Underwater survey

Accredited underwater surveyor of the Maritime Industry Authority

Our divers inspect the parts of a ship below the waterline while she is afloat. A satisfactory survey supports the issue of a Certificate of Inspection under PMMRR '97 and later MARINA safety rules, or an Interim Certificate of Inspection that extends its validity, without dry-docking.
